## ✅ Summary
Resolved the “false success” defect by enforcing a strict state machine in **Fixed_Transfer_Flow_v2**: success is emitted only after **confirmed debit + credit**. Added retries, rollback, structured audit logging, mock ledger, and a five-case integration suite with a single-command runner. All tests pass.

---

## 🔧 Key Changes
- **State gating:** `FixedTransferFlowV2` only returns success after both legs confirm via `poll_status`.
- **Retries & timeouts:** Configurable posting/polling attempts; failures bubble up deterministically.
- **Rollback:** Automatic debit rollback on credit failure/timeout with audited outcome.
- **Structured audit logs:** JSON logs with `txn_id`, `correlation_id`, masked accounts (no sensitive exposure).
- **Mock ledger:** mock_ledger_service.py simulates async outcomes safely.

---

- controller.py – Orchestrates debit→credit with rollback & audits.
- state_machine.py – Legal transitions (INIT→DEBIT_PENDING→…→COMPLETED/ROLLED_BACK).
- mock_ledger_service.py – Scenario-driven ledger simulator.
- run_suite.py – Integration runner.
- transfer_cases.yaml – Five scenarios (success, posting failure, retry, timeout+rollback, audit).

- requirements.txt, setup.sh – Dependencies & bootstrap.

---

## 🔍 Lifecycle & Root Causes
| Stage | State | Risk | Fix |
|-------|-------|------|-----|
| Debit post | `DEBIT_PENDING` | Async post failure not propagated | Bounded retries, fail-fast |
| Debit confirm | `DEBIT_CONFIRMED` | Silent poll timeout | Poll gating; timeout → failure |
| Credit post | `CREDIT_PENDING` | Failure after debit | Rollback triggered |
| Credit confirm | `COMPLETED` | False success before confirm | Success gated on confirmed credit |
| Audit | — | No correlation, sensitive data leakage | JSON logs w/ masked accounts & IDs |

See detailed RCA in root_cause_analysis.md.
